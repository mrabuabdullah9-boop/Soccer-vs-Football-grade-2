02. Key Vocabulary

Look at each picture. Read and listen.

player

A person in a game.

kick

Hit the ball with your foot.

throw

Send the ball with your hand.

helmet

A hard hat for the head.

soccer

A game. Players kick the ball.

football

A game. Players can throw the ball.

03. Two Games

Look at the two games.

U.K. — Soccer

Players wear shorts and T-shirts.

The ball is black and white.

Players kick the ball.

U.S.A. — Football

Players wear helmets.

The ball is not black and white.

Players can throw the ball.

Remember: Soccer: kick. Football: throw.
